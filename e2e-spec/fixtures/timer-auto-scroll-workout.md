## Worlds Greatest Stretch

Step 1. Start in a high plank with hands under shoulders and a long, neutral spine from head to heels.
Step 2. Step the left foot forward to the outside of the left hand, keeping the back knee off the floor.
Step 3. Drop the hips toward the floor and feel the stretch through the front of the right hip.
Step 4. Place the right hand on the floor and drive the left elbow down toward the instep of the left foot.
Step 5. Pause for a slow breath, keeping the chest open and the back leg long and active.
Step 6. Rotate the left arm up toward the ceiling, following the hand with the eyes and opening the chest.
Step 7. Return the hand to the floor inside the left foot and straighten the front leg to stretch the hamstring.
Step 8. Keep the hips square and the spine long while the front toes point up toward the ceiling.
Step 9. Bend the front knee again, return to the lunge, and repeat the rotation with control.
Step 10. Step back to the high plank and repeat the full sequence on the other side.
Step 11. Move slowly and breathe through every position rather than bouncing into the stretch.
Step 12. Keep the core braced throughout so the lower back does not sag toward the floor.
Step 13. If the back knee needs support, lower it to the floor and keep the hips moving forward.
Step 14. Stop the movement if you feel pinching in the front of the hip rather than a stretch.
